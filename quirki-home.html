Rules & Guidelines

01
This is a space for original fiction
Quirki is built around original stories. That's what this community is here for.
02
No spam
We're anti-spam in every form — comments, reviews, forum posts, or anything that clutters the space for everyone else.
03
Engage honestly
Points exist to reflect genuine contribution. Coordinated likes, fake reviews, or any attempt to game the system will result in removal.
04
Discrimination has no place here
Quirki does not tolerate discrimination of any kind.
05
Quirki is 13+
We'd love to have you here — all we ask is that you register as 13 or older.
