Chapter 1
The First Forgetting
3,241 words by Zee

They said the forgetting was painless. Mira had believed them the first time. The second time, she'd believed them less. The third time, she'd stopped asking.

The scar on her left wrist was the newest. Still pink. She'd woken with it three weeks ago and spent the better part of an hour trying to remember what had been taken.

The ceremony hall smelled of cedarwood and cold iron. Forty-two people stood in rows ahead of her.

"First time?" the woman asked.

Mira looked at her wrist. "No."

The scales tipped before she touched them.

She noticed. She was almost certain no one else did.
