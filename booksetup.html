  • 1. Making a book You need the Book Builder to format and convert your book into the right file type.
  • 2. Getting it on site You need to import an EPUB file. If you already have one, upload it here. If not, the Book Builder will make one for you.
  • 3. Book covers Don't have a cover? No worries. The site automatically creates one using your name and book title.
  • 4. Editing limits You can preview and configure files here, but you cannot edit or reformat text on this screen. This page is only for choosing when and how to publish.
  • 5. Ongoing work When you are ready to add more chapters, click "Re-import Replacement EPUB" inside your book's Configurations menu. This will not delete what is already live on the site.
  • 6. Editing live books To change what is live on the site after replacing your file, click "Unupload" and then "Upload" again. Do not create a brand new duplicate book row.
  • 7. Deleting books You cannot delete a book yourself. Unpublish your chapters, replace with an empty EPUB, and email us to finalize deletion.
