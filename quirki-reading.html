How Tagging Works

Every story on Quirki is tagged by its author. Tags are the only way stories are discovered here — there is no algorithm. What you find is what authors have chosen to describe their work as. If a tag feels missing or misleading, you can suggest one and the author will be notified.

Search by genre, trope, theme, or any combination. The more specific, the closer you'll get to exactly what you're looking for.

Copyright & Your Rights as a Reader

Every story on this platform belongs to its author. Reading here is free and always will be — but reproducing, redistributing, or claiming someone else's work is a legal matter, not just a platform rule. Authors retain full copyright over everything they post.

Some authors have chosen to make their work available to save. Others have kept it in-app only. Respect whichever choice they've made.

Community

Don't like, don't read. Not your cup of tea? Don't drink.

Unsolicited critique results in a point reduction. If an author has turned off comments or reviews, that's their answer.

Genuine engagement — editing, feedback, interaction — can result in point gain.
